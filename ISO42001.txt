1. WHOLE-APP AIMS ARCHITECTURE & AI GOVERNANCE POSTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- AI System Policies & Boundaries (A.2):
  * The platform operates under a strict "Human-in-the-Loop" (HITL) and "Advisory-Only" policy.
  * No automated decision-making (ADM) executes irrevocable legal, financial, or visa actions without explicit user confirmation and verified human agent involvement.
  * Directives require all AI processes, skills, and agents to remain fully compatible with low-cost, high-efficiency models that allow MCP (Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, Claude Haiku 4.5, gpt-6-luna).

- Internal Organisation & Accountability (A.3):
  * Overall AI governance and model safety accountability rests with the Superadmin and Platform Lead.
  * Technical issues, hallucinations, or biased ranking reports can be escalated directly through the admin feedback loop.

- Resources for AI Systems (A.4):
  * Algorithms & Models in Scope:
    1. Distance-Proximity & Keyword Matching Engine (deterministic ranking model; src/lib/search.ts, src/lib/geo.ts).
    2. Bayesian Average Review Scorer (statistical rating model; src/lib/reviews.ts).
    3. Multi-Model Advisory Workflows (architecture specifications for Gemini Flash-Lite, Claude Haiku, open-weights; src/data/cambodiaData.ts).
    4. Client-Side Image Crop Pre-processing (deterministic computer vision scaling; src/lib/images.ts).
  * Infrastructure: Google Cloud Platform / Firebase serverless edge; zero local model training or heavy GPU clusters, ensuring low carbon footprint and adherence to ISO/IEC 27001 Amendment 1:2024.

- Assessing Impacts of AI Systems (A.5 / Clauses 6.1.4 & 8.4; ISO/IEC 42005):
  * Individual Impact: Low risk. The application does not issue visas or deny entry; it recommends accredited agents, accommodation, and official government links.
  * Group / Bias Impact: Minimized. Ranking algorithms combine distance (50%), query relevance (30%), and verified reviews (20%) deterministically without demographic, racial, or nationality scoring bias.
  * Societal Impact: Positive economic contribution by connecting local Cambodian merchants, drivers, translators, and verified agents with international digital nomads and tech founders.

- AI System Life Cycle & Technical Guardrails (A.6 & A.9):
  * Schema-Locked I/O: All inputs and outputs adhere to strict Zod and JSON schema contracts (`additionalProperties: false`) preventing hallucinated field injection (data-contracts.MD).
  * Rate-Limiting & Cost Caps: Supporter tokens use strict positive integers; external API calls (e.g. Frankfurter FX, ImgBB) are throttled and cached to prevent resource exhaustion.
  * Graceful Fallbacks: Search defaults to full category listing if query tokens are empty or unrecognized; FX defaults to 4,050 KHR if network fails.

- Data for AI Systems (A.7):
  * Provenance: Official legal, visa, and corporate registration data sourced exclusively from Cambodian government portals (CamDX, FPCS, Ministry of Foreign Affairs, MPWT).
  * Privacy: Zero private user messages or personal relocation details are fed into third-party AI training sets.

- Information for Interested Parties (A.8):
  * Transparency: Algorithmic ranking weights (proximity 0.5, service 0.3, reviews 0.2) are published and documented openly in source code (src/lib/search.ts) and system ledgers, conforming to Australian Privacy Act ADM transparency principles.
  * Content Disclosures: Community advisory guides disclose technical architectures and AI agent integration patterns clearly.

- Third-Party & Vendor Ecosystem (A.10):
  * Cloud infrastructure provided by Google Cloud Platform.
  * Payments handled by Stripe; images handled by ImgBB.
  * Model recommendations explicitly specify provider boundaries (Google Gemini API, Anthropic Claude API) without client credential exposure.
